Independent poster project

Whalebone City

In a future where ocean pollution has wiped out marine life, people build their last refuge inside a whale’s skeleton.

My role
Concept and poster design

Design process

The starting point

I began this project during the public debate over Japan’s release of treated water from the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear power plant. The debate led me to ask what would happen if we destroyed the marine ecosystems we depend on.

Fukushima discharge context (IAEA)

From whale to city

I imagined people who had run out of places to live moving into a whale’s remains. The ribs frame the spaces they shelter in, and the spine gives the settlement its shape. The whale is both a trace of what people destroyed and the structure that keeps them alive.
